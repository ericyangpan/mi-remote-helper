- The app bundles the original image; SwiftUI crops the device and button area. Outlines use original-image pixel coordinates and scale with the photo. The photo does not accept clicks or send button actions.
- The crop is `(920, 490, 260, 622)`, with center axis `x = 1050`. Voice and Mappings share a 220 pt presentation column and a maximum photo width of 164 pt. The photo is centered above a fixed-height feedback label so label changes do not shift it.
- Ordinary presses use blue outlines. Non-voice buttons held for 0.5 seconds use a thicker orange outline and a long-press label. Release or cancellation clears the held state. Reduce Motion disables animated transitions.
- Outlines are vector paths with a thin white border and no glow. Power, Voice, OK, Back, Home and Menu are circles. Volume and TV use slightly elliptical outlines to fit the photo; directional buttons use ring sectors.
